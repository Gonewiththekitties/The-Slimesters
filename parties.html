What happens at the party?

At a Slime Bar Party

  1. Choose your prepared slime.
  2. Pick 3 toppings and 2 charms.
  3. Choose an optional scent.
  4. Mix, stretch, and make it yours!
  5. Pack it in your jar and take home the fun.

At a Slime-Making Party

  1. Get ready with your mixing supplies.
  2. Make your slime step by step with our host.
  3. Customize it with 3 toppings, 2 charms, and optional scent.
  4. Mix, stretch, and enjoy your creation.
  5. Pack it in your jar and take home your care card.

Party, Shipping & Safety Policies

Deposit: $75 non-refundable deposit after availability confirmation; it applies toward the party total. Remaining balance is due 7 days before the party.

Rescheduling: With at least 7 days' notice, the deposit may transfer one time to another available date. Cancellations within 7 days are non-refundable because supplies may already be prepared. Severe weather or unsafe travel may be rescheduled without a rescheduling fee.

Safety: Slime is not edible. Ages 4+ with adult supervision. Avoid eyes and mouth, wash hands before and after play, and keep small charms/add-ins away from children who may put them in their mouths.

Shipping: Shipping is available. Local pickup is available in Northeast Florida.
