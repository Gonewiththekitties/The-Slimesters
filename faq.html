FAQ & SLIME CARE

From parties to products, care instructions and more.

💗 What ages is The Slimesters for?

Our slime experiences are designed for ages 4+. Younger Slimesters may need a little extra grown-up help.

💙 What kind of slime do you make?

Butter slime and cloud slime. Both are soft, stretchy and fun to customize with colors, toppings, scents and themes.

🧡 What comes in Build Your Own Slime?

Each slime is 8 oz. Choose butter or cloud, color, glitter or Bingsu beads, a theme/charms and a scent.

🚫 Is slime edible?

No. Slime is for playing, not eating. Keep slime away from mouths and supervise children.

💚 Sensitive skin?

Ingredients or fragrance may cause sensitivity for some people. Choose unscented slime when preferred and supervise play.

👕 Does slime stain?

Slime can potentially stain clothing, carpet and other surfaces. Play on a protected surface and wear play clothes.

🫙 How do I store my slime?

Keep it in its airtight container at room temperature, away from direct heat and sunlight.

💧 What if my slime gets sticky?

Adult: mix 1 teaspoon borax into 1 cup warm water, stir until dissolved and let cool. Add only a few drops at a time and knead. Too much can make slime stiff. Label the activator and do not ingest.

☁️ What if my slime gets stiff?

For butter slime, knead with clean, dry hands; if needed, try a tiny amount of lotion or a few drops of water. For cloud slime, knead and let it rest; a few drops of water may help.

📦 Do you ship?

Yes, shipping is available. Local pickup is also available in the Northeast Florida area.

🚗 Where do you travel for parties?

We travel throughout Northeast Florida and Southeast Georgia. Travel fees may apply depending on location.

💵 Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A $75 non-refundable deposit reserves your date and time after availability is confirmed and applies toward your party total.

Cancellation & rescheduling: You may reschedule at least 7 days before the event and transfer your deposit one time to another available date. The deposit is non-refundable. Cancellations within 7 days are non-refundable because supplies may already be prepared. Severe weather or unsafe travel can be rescheduled to another available date without a rescheduling fee.
